    What is Saju?

    Saju (사주) means "four pillars." Koreans have read it for centuries before big life moments: dating, marriage, a new job. Your birth year, month, day and hour each turn into two characters, eight in all, and together they map the energies you were born with.

    Peach Blossom (桃花) is the attraction star. Taeo finds it in your chart, tells you what kind of charm you have, and when it blooms.

    We convert your birth time to the local solar time of your birthplace, the way a Korean master would, so readings stay accurate wherever you were born.
